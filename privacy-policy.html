Effective Date: November 21, 2026 · Entity: my charm (“my charm studio”)

This Privacy Policy explains how my charm and its internal publishing application my charm studio collect, use, and protect information when interacting with our website and the Pinterest API v5.

1. Purpose & Scope of Pinterest API Usage

my charm studio is a single-owner, internal content management and analytics tool used solely to manage the official my charm Pinterest Business account (@hamdamaqbool367). It connects to the Pinterest API v5 to:

  • Read and organize boards and board sections owned by @hamdamaqbool367.
  • Create and schedule original Pin graphics with titles, descriptions, alt text, and curated destination links.

2. Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal data belonging to third-party Pinterest users or website visitors. Our website and Pinterest Pins may link to third-party retail partners and affiliate networks. Clicking an external product link will take you to a third-party retailer whose own privacy and cookie policies govern your visit. We clearly disclose affiliate relationships in accordance with FTC guidelines (see our Affiliate Disclosure).

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Because my charm studio is used exclusively for internal brand publishing, no external user accounts or visitor profiles are maintained. You may revoke Pinterest API access at any time from your Pinterest account settings under Security & Apps. For any privacy questions or deletion requests, email us at hamdamaqbool367@gmail.com.
